## Admission rule

If any external creative asset is added later, it is **not** covered by the statement above and must be individually recorded before release with:

- original/authoritative source URL;
- author/publisher;
- retrieval date;
- screenshot of source page;
- screenshot/copy of CC0 license evidence;
- license text or official license-page copy;
- original filename;
- SHA-256;
- intended use.

Missing information means `Pending Evidence`, not Approved. Any non-CC0 external creative asset remains outside the project's approved asset policy unless the policy itself is explicitly revised before submission.
